Systems

Each of these is live, and each is written up the same way: what it is actually for, the shape of the data underneath it, the pipeline drawn step by step, and the thing that went wrong badly enough to change the design. The diagrams are the argument; the prose explains why the arrows go that way.

Mania Group Retail operations · Data model Supplier spreadsheets normalised into products and size-level variants, an order whose fulfilment and payment are two independent facts, and a payment callback that cannot pay twice. → EPRIS Journal Editorial infrastructure · Localisation A background queue that fingerprints the translatable part of every record, re-translates only what has genuinely fallen behind, and reports honestly when it cannot. → EPRIS Museum Virtual museum · Curation and engineering Public-domain painting pulled over IIIF, re-encoded and held locally, hung in halls that are stable addresses rather than positions in a scene. → TeachEd Learning systems · Product and technology Teaching material modelled as structured lesson objects, so boards, generated worksheets and thirty classroom games are views of one thing rather than fifty separate editors. → Museum of Donetsk Photography Public archive · Collection and interface A three-stage pipeline that de-duplicates by image content, gives each photograph a passport of date, place, author and licence, and refuses to hang anything whose provenance is unclear. → Winnipeg Nights Real-time media · Session coordination One-to-many WebRTC audio coordinated through an ordinary polled data store instead of a signalling service, with a self-refreshing news feed and on-the-fly speech translation on the same single-core host. → ARM CRM Sales operations · Interface placement A lead intake that scores and assigns on arrival, and a CRM placed inside an existing messenger so that recording a conversation happens where the conversation happens. → Munister Agency Temporal-geographic computation Working-hour overlap computed per US state from the platform's built-in IANA rules, so daylight saving resolves itself, over a 32 kb geometry model that keeps the page static. → Blue Ferret Publishing · CMS and operations A CMS on the live server whose content is snapshotted, diffed and committed to version control every thirty minutes, giving editors an undo history nobody has to remember to create. → AM Publishing Publishing operations · Distributed services Editorial metadata kept independent of sales channels, with transactional and streaming services separated out so that catalogue, payment and radio can fail without each other. →
