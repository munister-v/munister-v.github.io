One relational model, drawn once

The same model the article argues about, laid out as tables you can open. Every field carries the reason it exists, every table names who is allowed to write it, and the five invariants sit against the rows they actually hold. Nothing here is a screenshot of an admin panel: it is the shape of the data, which is the part that decides everything else.

countedWritten by a person or a document. The only numbers anybody actually observed.
derivedComputed from rows that own the truth. Never typed, and rebuildable from scratch.
identityUsed to attach an incoming row to a variant. Never generated by us.
Nothing selected

Six tables, and the whole argument of the system is in which of them owns a number. Open one: the variant is the only quantity anybody counted, the movement is the account of why it changed, and the product carries a total it is not allowed to type.

Invariantswhat holds, and what holds it
I1The product total equals the sum of its active variants after every committed movement.Recomputation inside the same transaction as the movement.
I2Every change of a quantity leaves exactly one journal row, carrying its cause.A single write path: sale, import and hand correction call one function.
I3No value used for matching is ever generated by us.Omission: the barcode generator is never run, and the size code is computed on read.
I4Every reported valuation carries the step that produced it.The resolver returns provenance with the number.
I5An ambiguous row is held. It is never completed with a default.The absence of a fallback in the parser.
