Irpin City for Android

A beta for Android 8.0 and later. It is not on Google Play yet: install the APK file from this page.

Version 0.1.0 · 78 MB · 27 November 2026

Air raid alerts: this version will not wake your phone

The Android version sends no air raid alert notifications. You see the status in the open app; the widget refreshes about every 15 minutes and can lag behind. Rely on sirens and official alert apps.

How to install

  1. Download the file to your phone and open it.
  2. Android asks you to allow installing from this source (your browser or file manager). Allow it; you can switch the permission off afterwards.
  3. Tap Install. The phone may warn you that the app is not from Google Play and offer to scan it.

The app does not update itself. Download a new version here and install it over the old one: the data on your phone stays.

Not yet here compared with iPhone

  • Notifications about air raid alerts and report status.
  • The QR code scanner and the service centre ticket with a barcode.
  • The audio guide and elevation profiles on routes.
  • Several homes and an encrypted wallet backup.
  • A separate no-network mode. Tariffs and reference data change only with a new version.

This is the first version and it has had little testing on real phones. Please report problems to support.
