Support

Irpin City is made by one person. Write with any question, a data error or an idea: it is read and answered, usually within a few days.

Write to the developer munister@outlook.com

Questions

The air raid alert did not arrive.

Notifications are a convenience and can be late or missing (no connection, iOS limits, a service failure). Always rely on sirens and official channels. In Settings, turn on “Air raid alerts and all-clear”, allow notifications and keep “Background App Refresh” on. Alerts are fetched from public sources in the background, so they need an internet connection and may arrive with a delay.

A venue, address, opening hours or a shelter is wrong.

Most data comes from OpenStreetMap. Use “Report a data error” on the venue or office page, or fix it at openstreetmap.org: after the next data update the change appears in the app.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

In the app: Settings → Report a problem or an idea. It opens an email to the developer; you can attach a screenshot and choose whether to include technical details (app and iOS version, device model, language, permissions). Nothing is sent without you pressing Send.

Why does the app ask for location?

Only to show where you are, the nearest shelters, stops and venues, and to navigate a route. It is computed on the phone and never sent to a server. Everything works without it.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset settings, Utilities → Set up → Erase all utilities data, or simply delete the app. Nothing is stored on a server.

Does navigation work with the screen locked?

Yes. While a route is running the app keeps location and voice prompts active and stops them when you finish.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later (the glass tab bar is used on iOS 26, the standard system bar on older versions). The interface is available in Ukrainian and English.
