This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Irpin City, an iPhone app published by Vyacheslaw Munister. It covers the app itself, the optional push notification service and the outside services the app connects to. It does not cover websites you open from the app: their own policies apply there.

Summary

  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you enter in the app (documents, meters, payments, your home, the emergency card) is stored only on your iPhone. The developer never receives it.
  • Your location is used only on the phone and is never sent to the developer.
  • If you turn on push notifications for air raid alerts or metro service changes, the developer’s server stores an anonymous push token for your device. Nothing else.
  • To show maps, timetables, alerts and the power schedule, the app requests public data from outside services. They see your IP address, as any website would.
  • You can delete everything at any time by turning the notifications off and deleting the app.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Vyacheslaw Munister, the publisher of the app. For any question about this policy or your data, write to munister@outlook.com. You will receive a reply in English or Ukrainian, usually within a few days and no later than 30 days.

What “personal data” means here

Personal data is any information that identifies you or could be linked to you, such as your name, address, location, documents or a device identifier. Technical data that cannot be linked to you (for example, which map tiles were requested) is not personal data on its own.

Data stored only on your iPhone

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

DataWhat it includes
WalletDocument names, numbers and expiry dates (no photos of documents), cars, insurance and inspection dates, meters and readings, payments, personal account numbers, your own services and phone numbers

Backups. iOS may include this data in your iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. Those backups are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The developer has no access to them.

Face ID and passcode. If you lock the wallet, iOS checks your face or passcode and only tells the app whether the check succeeded. The app never receives biometric data.

Data the developer receives

The developer receives personal data in only one case: when you turn on push notifications for air raid alerts or for Kyiv metro service changes, in a version of the app where the push service is enabled.

What is sentWhyHow long it is kept
A push token: a random identifier issued by Apple for this app on this deviceTo deliver the notification to your deviceUntil you turn the notifications off, or until Apple reports the token as invalid (for example, after you delete the app)
Your choices: alerts on or off, all-clear on or off, metro notices on or offTo send only the notifications you asked forSame as the token

The token does not contain your name, email, phone number or location, and the developer cannot use it to identify you. The push service does not record your IP address. The data is stored on a server rented by the developer and is used for nothing else. The only party that receives it is Apple, which delivers the notifications (Apple Push Notification service).

When the push service is not enabled or you leave these notifications off, alerts are checked by the app itself in the background and nothing is sent to the developer.

Each request reveals your IP address and ordinary technical details (time, app version, device type), as any internet connection does. The developer does not receive these requests and has no access to what the services log.

Links. Buttons such as “Buy a ticket”, “Queue online”, “Open in Maps” or links to institutions, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia and DTEK open those websites or apps. What happens there is governed by their own privacy policies.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it (“While Using the App”), the app uses it to show where you are, to find the nearest shelters, stops and venues, and to guide you along a route. The position is processed on the phone. It is not stored, not sent to the developer and not shared with any service listed above.

Navigation with the screen locked. When you start navigation on a walking or bike route, the app keeps receiving your location and plays voice prompts while the screen is locked. This is why the app declares background location and background audio. Both stop as soon as you end the route, and iOS shows the location indicator while they are active.

Reporting a problem

“Settings → Report a problem or an idea” opens a new email in your own mail app, addressed to the developer. It contains the text you write, a screenshot only if you attach one, and, unless you switch it off, technical details: app version, iOS version, device model, language and which permissions are granted. Nothing is sent until you press Send yourself. Emails are kept in the developer’s mailbox for as long as needed to answer and fix the issue, and you can ask for them to be deleted.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the features you see in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or training of any model, and it is not sold or rented to anyone. The app does not track you across apps or websites, as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency.

Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share personal data with third parties. The only exception is the push token, which Apple receives in order to deliver notifications; Apple processes it under its own privacy policy and the Apple Developer Program terms. The outside services listed above receive requests directly from your device, not from the developer, and only the technical data needed to answer them.

Security

Data on the device is protected by iOS encryption and, if you choose, by the wallet lock. The push service accepts connections only over HTTPS, requires an app key and stores nothing beyond what is listed above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the less data exists, the less can be exposed, which is why the app keeps almost everything on your phone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children, and it does not collect personal data from anyone except the push token described above.

International transfers

The outside services and Apple may process requests on servers outside Ukraine and the European Union. The developer does not transfer your personal data abroad, except that the push token is delivered to Apple.

Your rights

Under the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and, if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • know whether your data is processed and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights; in the EU, the data protection authority of your country.

Because the developer keeps no data that identifies you, a request can usually be met by the steps in “Retention and deletion”. For anything else, write to munister@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published at munister.com.ua/irpin/privacy and in the app (“Settings → About”) with a new date. If a change means the app starts collecting new data, it will ask for your consent before doing so.
