Privacy Policy

Irpin City · effective 2 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and your documents, meters, address and emergency card stay on your phone only. The developer does not see or collect them.

Who is responsible

The publisher is Vyacheslaw Munister, munister@outlook.com. Contact this address with any questions about data.

What is stored on your phone

Everything below stays in the app’s storage on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Wallet: document expiry dates, numbers and dates (no photos of documents), cars, meters, payments and personal accounts, custom services, my home, my Nova Poshta locker, your own phone numbers.
  • Emergency card: blood type, allergies, a close contact, medications and their reminders, go-bag.
  • Preferences: theme, language, map layers, widgets, saved places.

This data may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), depending on your iOS settings. The developer has no access to backups. You can delete the data in the app (“Settings → Reset settings”, “Utilities → Set up → Erase all utilities data”) or by deleting the app.

Wallet protection

Face ID or the passcode is checked by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data - only a yes or no answer.

Network requests

The app contacts outside services, which see your IP address and technical request details, as on any internet connection:

  • tiles.openfreemap.org - map tiles (the coordinates of the visible tiles are requested);
  • siren.pp.ua - air raid alert status for the Irpin community (without your location);
  • raw.githubusercontent.com - the open power outage schedule (outage-data-ua project);
  • t.me (public web view of the official Kyiv Metro channel) - metro service change notices, without your location;
  • api.open-meteo.com - terrain elevation for the points of a bike or walking route you open (route coordinates only, without your location);
  • overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee - only when you tap a building missing from the built-in data: that building’s coordinate is sent;
  • links to institutions’ websites, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia, Apple Maps and so on open in the browser or the relevant app, and their own policies apply.

The developer neither sees nor stores these requests.

Push notifications for alerts

If you turn on “Air raid alerts and all-clear” in a build with the push service enabled, your device sends our server:

  • an anonymous Apple Push token (a technical identifier of the device for this app);
  • two settings: whether to send the all-clear, and the build type (test or regular);
  • the app version.

No name, email, location or other identifier is sent. The data is used only to notify you when an alert starts and ends in the Irpin community, and is shared with no third party except Apple (push delivery). The token is deleted when you switch the toggle off, and automatically when Apple reports the device as invalid. Without this service alerts come from iOS background refresh and nothing is transmitted.

Route navigation

When you start navigation on a bike or walking route, the app uses your location to show you on the track, calculate distance and time and announce turns by voice. Navigation keeps working with the screen locked (which is why the app declares background location and audio modes for voice prompts). Your position is processed only on the phone: it is not stored, not sent to any server and not shared with third parties. Location is switched off when navigation ends.

Reporting a problem

The “Report a problem” form creates an ordinary email in your own mail app, addressed to the publisher. It contains only the text you wrote, optionally a screenshot you attached yourself, and (if you leave the switch on) technical details: the app version, iOS version, device model, language and permission state. No name, address, location or personal data. You send the email yourself; the app transmits nothing in the background.

Children

The app is not designed to collect data about children and collects nothing.

Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing to ask to be deleted or corrected. If you turned on push and want the token gone from the server for certain, switch the toggle off in the app or write to the address above. You have the rights provided by Ukraine’s Law on Personal Data Protection and, for EU residents, the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app (“Settings → Legal”) with a new date.
