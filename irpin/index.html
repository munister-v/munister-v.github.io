Irpin City

A map with building heights and shelters, trains and minibuses, a power outage schedule, utilities, documents and an emergency card: everything about Irpin in one app. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Coming to the App StoreSupport

What it does

Map

Venues, shelters, stops, cycle paths and 3D building heights, “open now”, search by street or venue, and a map you can restyle: colour themes, label density, building tone.

Transport

Trains from Irpin with a countdown and reminders, minibuses to Kyiv, Bucha and Hostomel with the route on the map and a time estimate, the Kyiv metro with service-change notices, Ukrzaliznytsia tickets.

Air raid alerts

Status in the community, the nearest shelter and route to it, instant notifications for an alert and the all-clear, and statistics for the last weeks.

Power

The DTEK outage schedule for your queue, a reminder 30 minutes before, and a dashboard with the day’s outage hours.

Walks and bike routes

47 walking, running and cycling routes with elevation profiles, surfaces, what is along the way and GPX export, plus turn-by-turn navigation with voice prompts.

Literary Irpin

Sights, a literary mural, the Writers’ Creative House and Ukrainian authors tied to the town: Rylsky, Stelmakh, Tychyna and more.

Wallet and utilities

Documents with expiry dates, meters, payments, cars, an emergency card and a go-bag, locked with Face ID, and an estimated monthly utility bill. Everything stays on the phone.

Services and phone book

Service centre and Diia guides, emergency numbers, the town’s offices and your own numbers.

Private by design

No accounts

There is nothing to sign in to. The app works without registration.

Data stays on your phone

Documents, meters, address and the emergency card are never sent anywhere. The developer does not see them.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.

Please note

Irpin City is not an official app of the city council, DTEK, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia or any other institution. It does not replace sirens or official warning systems. Timetables, tariffs and opening hours come from open sources: check before you travel.
