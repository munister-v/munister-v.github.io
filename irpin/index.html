Irpin City

A map with building heights and shelters, trains and minibuses, a power outage schedule, utilities, documents and an emergency card: everything about Irpin in one app. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Coming to the App StoreSupport

01

Today

Everything you check in the morning, on one screen.

  • Next train to Kyiv with a countdown
  • How many venues are open, and when it peaks
  • Nearest shelter and your power outage queue

02

Map

A calm night map that shows only what you ask for: building heights in 3D, shelters, stops and venues. Change the colour theme, label density and building tone, or switch layers off.

  • Floor counts for every building
  • “Open now” filter and search by street
  • Restyle it in two taps

03

Transport

Minibuses and trains with the route drawn on the map and an honest time estimate for every stop.

  • Trains from Irpin with a countdown and reminders
  • Minibuses to Kyiv, Bucha and Hostomel
  • Kyiv metro with service-change notices

04

Walks and bike routes

47 routes with elevation profiles, surfaces and what lies along the way. Start navigation and follow turn prompts, by voice if you like.

  • Elevation, surface and water points on the way
  • GPX export
  • Navigation works with the screen locked

05

Literary Irpin

The town’s Ukrainian writers, the Creative House, a mural of fourteen classics. Descriptions are links, so one sight leads to the next.

  • 15 authors, from Rylsky to Kostenko
  • Sights with photos and routes
  • Every description links onwards

06

Services and phone book

Emergency numbers, the town’s offices and your own contacts, plus guides to the service centre and Diia.

  • Call with one tap, with confirmation
  • Save the numbers you need on top
  • Utilities, documents and an emergency card stay on your phone

Private by design

No accounts

There is nothing to sign in to. The app works without registration.

Data stays on your phone

Documents, meters, address and the emergency card are never sent anywhere. The developer does not see them.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.

Please note

Irpin City is not an official app of the city council, DTEK, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia or any other institution. It does not replace sirens or official warning systems. Timetables, tariffs and opening hours come from open sources: check before you travel.
