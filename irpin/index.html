Irpin City

Everything about the town, in one calm app.

Trains and minibuses with honest times, a map with every building’s height and the nearest shelter, the DTEK outage schedule for your queue, utility bills and documents. Made for people who live in Irpin.

Home screen

Your morning, on one screen.

Four live widgets over a night map of Irpin: the next train, how busy the town is, the nearest shelter and your power queue. Below them is what is close to you right now.

Next train
Departure to Kyiv with a countdown and the fare, from 30 ₴. One tap opens the booking.
Open venues
How many places are open now, with a curve of the day that shows when the town gets busy.
Nearest shelter
Distance and address of the closest shelter, and the walking route to it.
Nearby
The closest pharmacy, ATM, Nova Poshta locker and branch, and drinking water.

Map

A map that shows only what you ask for.

A quiet night map built on OpenStreetMap and MapLibre. Buildings are raised by floor count, layers switch on and off, and a selected place opens in a card without leaving the map.

Building heights
Every building in 3D and coloured by its number of floors, from 1 to 17 and above.
Shelters and stops
Shelter clusters, minibus stops and the station, with the distance from you.
Open now
Filter 451 venues by category and by what is open at this minute, or search by street.
Your style
Colour theme, label density and building tone. Tiles are cached for use without a connection.

Transport

Trains and minibuses, with honest times.

Current timetables, routes drawn on the map and a time estimate for every stop. The estimate uses the average trip speed and the number of stops, so it does not promise what the road cannot give.

Trains
Departures from Irpin with a countdown and reminders. The Ukrzaliznytsia booking opens with Irpin, Kyiv-Pasazhyrskyi and the date already filled in.
Minibuses
392, 395, 100, 6616 and other routes to Kyiv, Bucha and Hostomel: interval, fare, length, stops and travel time.
Near you now
The closest stop and the next departures from it, without opening a timetable.
Kyiv metro
The line map and service-change notices, so you know before you transfer.

Wallet and utilities

Your home’s paperwork, on the phone.

Bills, meters and documents in one place, locked with Face ID and never sent anywhere. The app works out what you owe this month from your readings and the town’s current tariffs.

Utility estimate
Electricity 4.32 ₴ per kWh (2.16 at night), gas 7.96 ₴ per m³, water and sewerage 82.42 ₴ per m³, heating and waste.
Readings and payments
Reminders for the reading window and the payment deadline, a payment log and a six-month chart. Account numbers copy in one tap.
Documents
Passport, driving licence, insurance: expiry dates with reminders 30, 7 and 1 day before.
Home and emergency card
Address, entrance, nearest shelter and parcel locker; a medical card and contacts; cars with insurance and inspection dates.

Service centre

Literary Irpin

The town the classics wrote in.

From 1936 Irpin had a House of Creativity among the pines, where Rylsky, Tychyna, Stelmakh and Dovzhenko worked. The app tells the story of 15 writers who lived and wrote here, with sources.

Writer of the day
A new Irpin writer every day on the home screen, with a portrait engraving, a tagline and a link to their place on the map.
Long reads
A life story in chapters for each of the 15 writers, a quote with its source, and what connects them to Irpin.
A literary walk
A route past the House of Creativity, the library and the mural of fourteen classics, with the writers’ places on the way.
Widgets
Writer of the day as a home-screen widget, in three sizes.

A closer look

A few screens in detail: what is on them and why they are there.

A place, with its story

Every sight has a photo with its licence, facts at a glance and a short history. This one tells how, in February 2022, thousands of people left the town on foot under the ruined spans of the Romanivka Bridge. The underlined words lead to nearby places and routes.

Real photos, with their authors

Churches, parks, monuments and memorials come with open photographs from Wikimedia Commons. The author and the licence are printed on the picture, and below are the address, the community and the time from you on foot, by bike and by car.

Where Irpin began

The town grew up around a railway stop in the late 19th century. The page says which towns lie on the line, how long the train to Kyiv-Pasazhyrskyi takes (37-59 minutes) and what a ticket costs, from 30 ₴.

The map and a card

Tap a place and a card slides up from below, with its category, photo and distance, and the map stays where it was. Filters across the top: all venues, open now, coffee.

A minibus route, honestly

Interval, fare, length, number of stops and the travel time, which the app calculates from average speed and says outright that it is an estimate. The route and every stop are drawn on the map.

A walk: Literary Irpin

2.2 km, about 30 minutes, easy. The route runs from the House of Creativity through the writers’ park to the embankment. An elevation profile, numbered places on the map and turn-by-turn guidance.

A walk: the Battle for Irpin

A memorial route past the places where the defence of Kyiv Oblast was held in March 2022: the memorial sign, the T-34 tank and the mass graves. 1.5 km, about 20 minutes, with an elevation profile.

66 sights

Filter by history, nature or culture. At the top is Literary Irpin: 15 Ukrainian authors, Parnassus, the mural and the prize. Each tile opens a place with a photo and a story.

A writer you can read

A portrait engraving, a short bio and a long read in chapters. Mykhailo Stelmakh, born in the village of Diakivtsi, wrote “The Great Family” and “Truth and Falsehood” and lived in Irpin from the early 1950s until his death.

On the home screen

The Writer of the Day widget in three sizes: a portrait, a name, a line about their link to Irpin. A new writer every day, and a tap opens their page in the app.

The town in numbers

Population, the year Irpin became a town (1956), the Hero City title (2022), the districts, and 61 residential complexes you can search and sort, each with buildings, floors, entrances and what is within walking distance.

Public services, without guessing

The service centre with its live status and an online queue button. Below it, life situations: registration, property and land, your own business, the birth of a child. Each one says where to go and what to bring.

A phone book that is always at hand

72 numbers: the emergency ones in red at the top (112, 103, 102), then town offices and utilities, and your own. Every call asks for confirmation, so you do not ring by accident.

451 places in numbers

Venues by category with counts (coffee 61, food 42, pharmacies 53), how many are open right now and how many have published opening hours. The data is OpenStreetMap, and you can correct it from the app.

Routes by type and distance

20 km of cycle paths: 17 bike routes and 33 on foot. Filter by surface, cycle path or forest, or sort by the nearest: each card has a map, length and time.

Alerts and power

Air raid alerts

The status for the Irpin community, a notification for the alert and the all-clear, the nearest shelter with a route, and how long alerts lasted today and in recent weeks. Notifications are a convenience: always rely on sirens.

Power outages

The DTEK Kyiv Oblast schedule for your sub-queue, from 1.1 to 6.2. The announced outages for today and tomorrow, the weekly template of possible ones, 48 half-hour slots and a reminder 30 minutes before the power goes.

Screens

Private by design

No accounts

There is nothing to sign in to. The app works without registration.

Data stays on your phone

Documents, meters, address and the emergency card are never sent anywhere. The developer does not see them.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.

Please note

Irpin City is not an official app of the city council, DTEK, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia or any other institution. It does not replace sirens or official warning systems. Timetables, tariffs and opening hours come from open sources: check before you travel.
