Irpin City

Everything about the town, in one calm app.

Trains and minibuses with honest times, a map with every building’s height and the nearest shelter, the DTEK outage schedule for your queue, utility bills and documents. Made for people who live in Irpin.

For iPhone · English and Ukrainian. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Personal data never leaves the phone.

Home screen

Your morning, on one screen.

Four live widgets over a night map of Irpin: the next train, how busy the town is, the nearest shelter and your power queue. Below them is what is close to you right now.

Next train
Departure to Kyiv with a countdown and the fare, from 30 ₴. One tap opens the booking.
Open venues
How many places are open now, with a curve of the day that shows when the town gets busy.
Nearest shelter
Distance and address of the closest shelter, and the walking route to it.
Nearby
The closest pharmacy, ATM, Nova Poshta locker and branch, and drinking water.

Map

A map that shows only what you ask for.

A quiet night map built on OpenStreetMap and MapLibre. Buildings are raised by floor count, layers switch on and off, and a selected place opens in a card without leaving the map.

Building heights
Every building in 3D and coloured by its number of floors, from 1 to 17 and above.
Shelters and stops
Shelter clusters, minibus stops and the station, with the distance from you.
Open now
Filter 451 venues by category and by what is open at this minute, or search by street.
Your style
Colour theme, label density and building tone. Tiles are cached for use without a connection.

Transport

Trains and minibuses, with honest times.

Current timetables, routes drawn on the map and a time estimate for every stop. The estimate uses the average trip speed and the number of stops, so it does not promise what the road cannot give.

Trains
Departures from Irpin with a countdown and reminders. The Ukrzaliznytsia booking opens with Irpin, Kyiv-Pasazhyrskyi and the date already filled in.
Minibuses
392, 395, 100, 6616 and other routes to Kyiv, Bucha and Hostomel: interval, fare, length, stops and travel time.
Near you now
The closest stop and the next departures from it, without opening a timetable.
Kyiv metro
The line map and service-change notices, so you know before you transfer.

Wallet and utilities

Your home’s paperwork, on the phone.

Bills, meters and documents in one place, locked with Face ID and never sent anywhere. The app works out what you owe this month from your readings and the town’s current tariffs.

Utility estimate
Electricity 4.32 ₴ per kWh (2.16 at night), gas 7.96 ₴ per m³, water and sewerage 82.42 ₴ per m³, heating and waste.
Readings and payments
Reminders for the reading window and the payment deadline, a payment log and a six-month chart. Account numbers copy in one tap.
Documents
Passport, driving licence, insurance: expiry dates with reminders 30, 7 and 1 day before.
Home and emergency card
Address, entrance, nearest shelter and parcel locker; a medical card and contacts; cars with insurance and inspection dates.

Public services

Paperwork without guessing.

The town’s service centre with its live status, and guides that say where to go, what to bring and what can be done online.

Service centre
Open or closed right now and how long until closing, the address, hours and the online queue.
12 life situations
Registration, property, sole trader, birth of a child, IDP status, pension: the office, hall and phone, and a document checklist.
Phone book
72 numbers: emergency services, town offices and utilities, plus your own. Every call asks for confirmation.
The town
61 residential complexes with buildings, floors and entrances, and what is within walking distance.

Walks and bike routes

50 routes, with navigation.

47 walks and 3 bike routes through Irpin, along the river and into the forest, with turn-by-turn guidance that keeps working with the screen locked.

Before you go
Distance, time, elevation profile, surface and drinking water on the way.
On the way
Turn prompts with distance, optional voice, the distance left, time on the way and arrival.
GPX export
Send any route to a watch or another app.
Places on the route
66 sights with photos and short histories, linked to each other and to the routes.

Alerts and power

Air raid alerts

The status for the Irpin community, a notification for the alert and the all-clear, the nearest shelter with a route, and how long alerts lasted today and in recent weeks. Notifications are a convenience: always rely on sirens.

Power outages

The DTEK Kyiv Oblast schedule for your sub-queue, from 1.1 to 6.2. The announced outages for today and tomorrow, the weekly template of possible ones, 48 half-hour slots and a reminder 30 minutes before the power goes.

Screens

Private by design

No accounts

There is nothing to sign in to. The app works without registration.

Data stays on your phone

Documents, meters, address and the emergency card are never sent anywhere. The developer does not see them.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.

Please note

Irpin City is not an official app of the city council, DTEK, Ukrzaliznytsia, Diia or any other institution. It does not replace sirens or official warning systems. Timetables, tariffs and opening hours come from open sources: check before you travel.
