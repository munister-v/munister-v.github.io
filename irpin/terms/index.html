1. What the app is

Irpin City is a guide to the town of Irpin, Ukraine: transport, a map, air raid alert status, the power outage schedule, utilities, public services, walking and bike routes, and a personal wallet stored on your phone. It is made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any public authority, utility or carrier mentioned in it.

2. Not a warning system or an official source

  • Air raid alerts. Alert status and notifications are an extra convenience. They can be delayed or not arrive at all because of the network, iOS limits or a failure of a data source. Always follow sirens and official channels and apps. Never rely on Irpin City alone for your safety.
  • Emergencies. In danger, call 112 (101 fire and rescue, 102 police, 103 ambulance). Information in the app is not medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Shelters. Shelter locations come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete or out of date. A marker does not guarantee that a shelter exists, is open or is suitable.
  • Power outages. The schedule is taken from the open outage-data-ua project, which collects DTEK announcements. DTEK can change it at any moment; its official channels take precedence.
  • Timetables, fares, tariffs, opening hours, addresses. They come from open and public sources and may contain errors or change without notice. Check with the carrier or institution before you travel or visit.
  • Utility amounts are estimates calculated from your readings and the tariffs known to the app. The amount you owe is the one on your supplier’s bill.
  • Routes and navigation. Routes and turn prompts are built from open map data and may be inaccurate or miss closures and hazards. Obey traffic rules and signs, watch the road and do not handle the phone while riding or crossing. You choose your route and are responsible for your safety.
