Screens and how they work

The main screens of the app on a real iPhone: what you see, how it works inside, and what changed in version 1.1. Screenshots use demonstration data: bookings, tickets and numbers are made up.

01Home

Your morning on one screen

The cards over the night map answer the day's questions: when is the train, what is open, where is the nearest shelter and is the power on.

How it works inside

Train
The next train to Kyiv with a countdown and a fare from 30 ₴. Computed on the phone from the timetable, on Kyiv time; if a train does not run daily, the card says so.
Venues
How many places are open now and the curve of the day. OpenStreetMap data, refreshed by separate reference sets without a release.
Shelter
The nearest, with distance and address. Location is used only on the phone and never sent anywhere.
Power
Your queue by the DTEK schedule: is the power on and when it changes. You pick the queue once.
New in 1.1
  • Train times and reminders are computed on Kyiv time even when the phone is in another time zone.

02Alert

When an air raid alert sounds

The app changes at once: the shelter nearby and the way to it come first. Service-centre reception pauses for the duration, but your booking is not lost.

How it works inside

One poller
The alert state is checked in a single place: every 30 s, and every 15 s during an alert. Home, the alerts screen and notifications all see the same state.
The booking is not cancelled
The Booking tab says reception is paused and will resume after the all-clear. The widget and Lock Screen show the same.
Statistics
How long alerts lasted per day and over weeks; data is merged with the cache for up to 35 days because the service returns only recent records.
Honest about limits
Notifications are a convenience, not a replacement for sirens. Without push the app checks in the background when iOS allows.
New in 1.1
  • Statistics are counted on the Kyiv day, not the phone's time zone.
  • An alert record the server stopped returning is closed automatically and no longer inflates the day's duration.

03Booking

My service-centre visits

The next visit as a large card: day, time, service and a countdown. One tap to the ticket, the route or the booking menu.

How it works inside

Check against the account
The app reads your queue account and confirms bookings, adds those you made on the site or at the terminal, and marks cancelled ones.
Reminders
A notification an hour before the visit and a Lock Screen card (Live Activity) two hours before. A cancelled booking does not remind.
History
Past visits stay in a separate tab: up to a year and a half or 200 records.
Several people
Several people can book from one phone: each signs in as themselves and bookings are kept apart.
New in 1.1
  • A booking moved in the account to another day or time is recognised by the site's record id: it is the same visit, not a 'missing' one plus a stranger.

04Queue sign-in

Sign in once, then no more

The electronic queue site opens in a window inside the app. The hints at the top show what to tap at each step.

Steps

  1. Tap “Sign in with ID.GOV.UA”.
  2. Choose a method: Diia.Signature, BankID or an electronic signature.
  3. Confirm the sign-in in Diia by QR code and return to the app.
  4. Check the data and confirm the booking on the page: the app sees the ticket by itself.

How it works inside

Sign-in is remembered
The queue site's cookies get a 30-day lifetime and persist across launches: the account and tickets open without signing in again.
Hints follow the site's rules
The words the app uses to recognise steps are a signed reference set: if the site changes a label, the set is updated, not the app.
We do not see your password
Sign-in happens on id.gov.ua. The app receives neither a password nor a key: it only reads the visible text of queue pages.
Sign out
“Sign out of the queue account” in the window menu erases everything the window remembered, including bank sessions.
New in 1.1
  • An account opened without a session leads to the sign-in page by itself and returns when you are in.
  • If the window was closed on the reservation page, the app opens the account instead of repeating the request: the time is not taken twice.

05Ticket

A ticket with a barcode that works offline

The same number, day, time and barcode as in the queue account, but at hand in the app.

How it works inside

The code, bar for bar
The queue site draws the barcode as dozens of black bars. The app reads their coordinates from the page markup and redraws the code four times sharper with a white margin: the scanner at the centre reads exactly what the queue issued.
Offline
The code lives on the phone. Tap it to show it full screen; brightness goes up while it is shown.
Official PDF
The file behind “Download ticket” is also saved as a backup and opens offline.
Found means not searched again
A ticket with its code is no longer re-read from the account each time you open it. Move or cancel it in the queue account.
New in 1.1
  • The barcode is taken from the account's markup, not from a window snapshot: before, the ticket number was read but the code was not.
  • The same works in the ordinary background check, with no window.

06Services

The town's directory in one place

State services, life situations, a phone directory, venues and routes. The service-centre card says whether it is open now.

How it works inside

Services
A catalogue of 75 service-centre services and a register of 516 state services. Some have booking into the electronic queue.
Life situations
12 scenarios: registration, property, a sole proprietorship, a birth, IDP status, pension… each with a document checklist.
Directory and town
72 phone numbers (emergency, offices, utilities), 451 venues, 61 housing complexes, 66 sights, 50 routes.
Outages nearby
A Power, Water, Gas block for residents' signals; off until you turn it on.

07Utilities

A bill from your readings, and outages nearby

An estimate for the month from your readings and current tariffs, reading and payment deadlines, a six-month chart.

How it works inside

Deadlines
Readings: from the second-to-last day of the month to the 3rd; payment: by the 20th. Reminders as you choose.
Outages nearby
A person taps “No power” (for power, only when the schedule says it is on). When several different devices in one area say the same, the area gets a notification.
What the server knows
Only an anonymous push token and a square of about 1 km; no name, number or exact coordinates. Signals are kept for up to a day.
Own services
Internet, intercom, security: separate lines that count both in the total and in the payment chart.
New in 1.1
  • A reading lower than the previous one (a meter replaced or a typo) no longer gives a negative total: the app says so.
  • Payments for own services now appear on the chart, and the bars match the month's “Paid” total.

08Transport

Trains and minibuses with honest times

A live train board, reminders and a ticket link with stations and date filled in. Minibuses to Kyiv, Bucha and Hostomel with route and stops.

How it works inside

Trains
On the way now and the next departures; a “not daily” mark; reminders for the time you choose, on Kyiv time.
Minibuses
392, 395, 100, 6616 and others: route, stops in order of travel and distance.
Metro
The Kyiv map and service-change notices.
Data
Routes from OpenStreetMap; what OSM does not draw is shown dashed as approximate.
New in 1.1
  • Routes 420, 379 and 99 no longer carry invented 7-14 km detours: OSM relation fragments lying far from the route were dropped.

09Map

A night map that shows only what you need

OpenStreetMap in a dark style: buildings in 3D by floors, shelters, stops, venues and cycle paths. Search by street or venue.

How it works inside

Filters
“All venues”, “Open now”, categories. Layers switch on when you need them.
Shelters
57 points in the community with level, address and a “for the institution only” restriction; a route to the nearest.
Navigation
Walking and cycling routes with your position; location is computed on the phone.

10Wallet

Documents, home and utilities only on the phone

Home, parcel locker, transport, medicines, documents with deadline reminders, cars with insurance and inspection dates, meters.

How it works inside

Only on iPhone
Storage protected by iOS and, if you wish, Face ID. The developer's server never sees this data.
Reminders
For document deadlines: at 30, 7 and 1 day. For readings and payment: on the days you choose.
Home
Address, entrance, the nearest shelter and parcel locker: the basis for personal cards on Home.

11Backup

A copy that cannot be opened without your password

Everything that lives only on the phone is packed into one encrypted file that you put wherever you like.

Steps

  1. Choose sections: wallet, documents, medicines, providers, contacts, saved items.
  2. Think of a password of 8 or more characters and create the copy.
  3. Save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or send it to yourself.
  4. On a new phone use “Restore from a copy”: the app takes a snapshot first, and the restore can be undone.

How it works inside

Encryption
AES-256-GCM, the key derived from the password with PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations). The file header is authenticated.
The password is stored nowhere
Forget it and the copy cannot be opened: the app says so plainly. It sends nothing anywhere.
Reminder
Once every three months if there is something to save; can be turned off.
New in 1.1
  • The password opens the file whichever way a letter such as “й” is encoded on different devices.
  • Copies you sent earlier are removed from the temporary folder so a file with your data is not left behind.

12Settings

Everything you can turn off or adjust

Notifications, appearance, language, trains, location and the legal texts in one list.

How it works inside

Notifications
Air raid alert and all-clear, outages nearby, metro changes, service-centre booking, Lock Screen, fact of the day. Each one separately.
Trains
Direction, daily trains only, how long before to remind.
Language
Ukrainian, English or as the system; switches at once, no restart.
Privacy
The policy, terms, licences and how it works, right in the app.

What the app does not do

Does not ask who you are

No accounts, ads or analytics. The wallet, tickets and barcodes stay on your phone.

Does not replace sirens

Alert and outage notifications are a convenience and can be late. Rely on sirens and official channels.

Is not a state app

It is an independent app: booking goes through the Diia queue site, and the ticket stays in the CNAP queue and your account.
