The Threshold of Time

An essay on doors as the most intimate surface a building offers to the street. It follows conservators who peel back layers of paint like stratigraphy, from the carved portals of Tbilisi to Ghiberti's Gates of Paradise, and argues that restoring a threshold is closer to archaeology than to carpentry: the wear in the stone records a habit of living that no census keeps.

8 July 2026 · Article · Author: Viacheslav Munister

The Sacred Spring: A Portrait of the Vienna Secession

Vienna at the end of the nineteenth century was a city dressed in borrowed history. On 3 April 1897 a group of painters and architects led by Gustav Klimt resigned from the Künstlerhaus and built a white temple crowned with gilded leaves. The essay follows what held them together: not a shared style but a conviction that every age deserves an art of its own.
