Where the Warmth Lives: How Two Traditions Measured the Turn from Summer to Autumn

Autumn does not make the light warmer. Measured region by region, two canvases of the same field show the sky staying where it was while the ground swings seventeen points warm, and the shadows, all summer filled with blue skylight, turn into the warmest passages in the picture. With three pastels in which the medium is held still and only the season moves. And a second half in which autumn is not a light at all: a calendar that declares it on the seventh of August, a season carried by one grass, and a moon with no horizon to rise from.
