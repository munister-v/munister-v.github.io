Viacheslav Munister

Writer, editor and heritage researcher working across long-form journalism, museum curation, editorial systems and product delivery.

Profile

I collect traces of places, objects and people, then give them a form that can be entered. Some become essays, some become archives, some become quiet digital instruments. At EPRIS Journal I write, interview, edit and curate: most of the journal’s published stories, its interviews and reviews, the complete Autumn 2026 edition and the EPRIS Museum. Alongside this authorial work, I lead product direction and delivery for complex editorial, educational, commerce and operational systems.
